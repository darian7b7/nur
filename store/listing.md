## Full description (max 4000)
"Allah is the ally of those who believe. He brings them out from darknesses into the light." (Al-Baqarah 2:257)

Nūr is a calm walking companion that helps you go outside, whatever the weather, whatever the mood.

Your day starts at night. As you walk, the sun rises over your path, and full light comes when you reach your daily goal. Along the way you find verses of the Quran and authentic hadith about walking, light, hope and patience: a new path every day, never the same two days running.

FOR THE HARDER DAYS
• Hard day: the goal becomes one small step outside the door.
• New way: one simple rule for a different route, so old places and old memories don't decide your walk.
• Weather: what to wear, and the Prophet ﷺ letting the rain fall on him, "because it has just come from its Lord".

QUIET BY DESIGN
• No sound, no ads, no account.
• Reads only your step count (Health Connect and the phone's step sensor). No location, no GPS, no battery drain.
• At most one gentle reminder a day, off unless you turn it on.
• No streaks and no guilt: each day starts fresh at dawn.

EVERY SOURCE, ONE TAP AWAY
Every verse opens on quran.com and every hadith on sunnah.com, with the Arabic, so you can always check the source. Translations of the meaning: Saheeh International and sunnah.com.

Nūr is free. If it brings you light, you can support it at ko-fi.com/darian7b7.
