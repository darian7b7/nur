Nūr

Privacy Policy · Last updated 21 October 2026

Nūr is a walking app. It is built to know as little about you as possible. Everything it keeps stays on your phone.

What Nūr reads

  • Your step count, from Health Connect (only after you allow it) and from your phone's step sensor while Nūr is open. Nūr uses it to show how far you walked today and to light up the sky.

Nūr does not read any other health data, your location, your contacts, your photos or anything else.

What Nūr keeps, and where

  • How far you walked each day, the verses and hadith you found, and your settings (daily goal, height for your stride, reminder time).
  • All of it is stored only on your phone. Nūr has no account, no server and no analytics, and sends nothing anywhere.

Sharing

Nūr does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone. It contains no advertising and no tracking.

Links

When you tap "Read on quran.com" or "Read on sunnah.com", that website opens in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Nūr schedules it on your phone. No reminder is sent from a server.

Deleting your data

In Nūr, open Settings and tap "Forget all walks". Uninstalling Nūr removes everything it kept. You can withdraw the step permission at any time in Health Connect or in your phone's settings.

Children

Nūr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
